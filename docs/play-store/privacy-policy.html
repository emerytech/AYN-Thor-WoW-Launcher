Who we are

WOW this game is FOREVER is an unofficial compatibility launcher published by ETS3D LLC / Emery Tech Solutions. For support or privacy questions, email hello@ets3d.com. We are not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment, Lossless Scaling, or the manufacturer of your device.

Files and settings on your device

The app imports a game folder only after you select it through Android’s folder picker. It copies the installation into app-specific storage. The original source folder is left unchanged. Imported files, controller settings and an optional frame-generation DLL are stored on the device. The app does not upload these files to ETS3D.

Optional saved login

You can choose to save a game login locally. The password is encrypted using Android Keystore. Automatic login and the Fill action provide the saved login to the imported game client so it can sign in to its account service. ETS3D does not operate that account service and does not receive the game login through a launcher backend. Game publishers process sign-in and game-service data under their own privacy policies.

Temporary clipboard copy

Copy password is an explicit action. It puts the password on Android’s clipboard and marks it sensitive to hide the system preview where supported. The app attempts to clear its own copy after 30 seconds while it can access the clipboard, or when it next regains access. A newer unrelated clipboard copy is left intact. Direct Fill does not use the Android clipboard. The app does not automatically submit a password after filling a selected field.

Network and microphone use

The Play edition does not download game installations or check GitHub for app updates. Updates to the app are handled by Google Play. Imported game software can contact its publisher and other services for authentication, gameplay and other features. If you grant microphone permission and use a supported voice feature, microphone audio can be made available to the game client. Review the privacy terms and settings of the game services you use. This policy does not describe or control the practices of independent game publishers.

Diagnostics and information you send us

Diagnostic logs may contain device information, file paths and error details. The launcher’s keyboard-input path does not log typed passwords. Review diagnostics before sharing them. If you email ETS3D or submit a public issue, you choose what information to send. We use support information to respond and troubleshoot. Do not include passwords, authenticator codes or other sensitive game-account information in support requests.

Deleting local data

Use Forget Saved Login to remove the launcher’s saved login. You can revoke microphone permission through Android settings. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes app-specific files and settings, including imported copies and saved credentials. Copies you keep elsewhere and accounts with game publishers are separate; contact the game publisher to delete a game account. Email hello@ets3d.com to request deletion of information you sent directly to ETS3D for support.
